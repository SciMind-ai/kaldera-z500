1. Introduction

Welcome to Kaldera Moto. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use our website. Kaldera Moto is a demonstration website. It does not provide user accounts, registration systems, contact forms, or comment sections. We do not directly request visitors to submit personal information through these features.

We do not claim to collect or maintain a personal database of visitors through this website.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that websites or third-party services may store on your device. They can be used for different purposes, including remembering preferences, measuring website performance, providing advertising, and understanding how visitors interact with online content.

Third-party services used on this website may use cookies, pixels, local storage, or similar technologies. These technologies may help advertising providers measure advertisements and improve their services.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect certain website or advertising features.

4. Advertising Services

Kaldera Moto may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including Adsterra.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies and other technologies to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraudulent activity, and understand general visitor interactions.

Depending on the advertising service and its configuration, information such as IP addresses, browser details, device information, and advertising identifiers may be processed by the provider or its partners.

We do not control every data-processing activity performed by external advertising companies. Visitors should review the privacy policies and applicable settings of those providers to understand their data practices.

5. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may use external services to provide certain features. These services may include Google Fonts for typography and cdnjs for loading JavaScript libraries and animation resources.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy information provided by external services when necessary.
